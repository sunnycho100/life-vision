Review guidance

A pending draft is not training data. Mark a source reviewed only after checking the visible evidence, then download the reviewed manifest.

visible_distress describes observable signs of distress. submerged_visible requires visible submerged body evidence. not_visible means the person is out of view or cannot be seen; it does not establish what happened off camera.

Use one stable, human-assigned target ID for a person across intervals and keyframes when identity is clear. Leave it blank when identity is unknown.

All data remains in this browser until you choose a downloaded file location. This page does not send videos or labels to a server.
